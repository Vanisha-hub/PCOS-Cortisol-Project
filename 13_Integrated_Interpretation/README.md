# Integrated Interpretation of Transcriptomic Findings

This folder contains **high-level integrative visualizations** that synthesize results from multi-dataset transcriptomic analyses conducted in the *PCOS–Cortisol Project*. The figures presented here are intended to **bridge statistical outputs with biological interpretation**, highlighting key molecular patterns, pathway convergence, and system-level insights relevant to PCOS pathophysiology.



## Folder Contents & Description

###  Differential Expression & Dataset Overview
- **SummaryDEGs.png**  
  Summary of differentially expressed genes identified across multiple datasets.

- **Graphs_SummaryDEGs.png**  
  Graphical overview of DEG overlap and trends across datasets and platforms.

- **summary_datasets.png**  
  Overview of datasets included in the analysis and their respective characteristics.

- **sample distribution across datasets.png**  
  Distribution of sample numbers across datasets used for integrated analysis.



###  Gene Family & Functional Representation
- **gene family counts.png**  
  Distribution of DEGs across major gene families.

- **gene family counts 137684.png**  
  Gene family representation specific to dataset GSE137684.



### Pathway & Systems-Level Integration
- **MetabolicVSEndocrineaxis.png**  
  Comparative representation of metabolic versus endocrine pathway involvement,
  highlighting systemic dysregulation in PCOS.

- **Pathway contribution of cortisol linked genes.png**  
  Pathway-level contribution of cortisol-associated genes identified in the analysis.

---

###  Network & Relationship Visualizations
- **PCOS_Circos_Enhanced.png**  
  Circos-based visualization illustrating relationships between genes, pathways,
  and functional categories associated with PCOS and cortisol signaling.

- **sankey diagra.png**  
  Sankey diagram representing flow and contribution of genes across datasets,
  functional categories, or pathways.

All figures in this folder are derived from analyses performed in above folders
